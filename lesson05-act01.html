Source Validation Lab

A cited answer is only as trustworthy as the sources behind it. In this lab you'll run a real Pro Search query in Perplexity, then click through and score every citation against a four-part credibility rubric — and decide whether the answer is something you'd be willing to stand behind in front of colleagues or students.

Step-by-step

Work through these in Perplexity (free tier includes a few Pro Searches per day). Check each box as you go — your progress is saved on this device.

Starter queries

Pick one and adapt it to your discipline. The point is a question with a checkable answer — not an opinion.

Template A — a current standard or figure

What is the current credit-hour definition used by my regional accreditor, and when was it last revised? Cite the primary source for each claim and note the publication date.

Template B — a contested claim worth triangulating

Summarize the current research consensus on [a debated topic in my field]. For each point, cite at least two independent sources and flag where the sources disagree.

Credibility scorer
