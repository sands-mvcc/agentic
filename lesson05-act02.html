Build a Verification Space

A one-off search forgets your standards the moment it ends. A Space — the workspace Perplexity used to call a Collection — remembers them. Here you'll stand up a project Space, give it grounding files, and use the builder below to write custom instructions that enforce your sourcing rules in every thread, so you never have to re-type "cite primary sources" again.
