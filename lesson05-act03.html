Scheduled Integrity Monitor

Research goes stale. A figure that was current when you cited it can quietly become wrong. In this activity you'll build a recurring Task that re-runs your query on a schedule and delivers a fresh, cited summary — then you'll practice the synthesis step itself, triangulating what multiple sources say in a structured matrix instead of trusting any single one.

Part A — Build the recurring Task

Tasks turn Perplexity from reactive to proactive: it runs on schedule and notifies you when a fresh, cited briefing is ready — so monitoring becomes a maintained record, not a one-time snapshot.

Part B — Synthesis matrix

When your Task (or a Deep Research run) returns several sources on one question, don't just take the top answer. Lay the claims side by side. Add a row per claim, note what each source says, and mark whether they agree. The matrix is saved on this device.
