Assistant Spec Sheet

The fastest way to build a bad assistant is to open a builder and start typing. The fastest way to build a good one is to decide, on paper, exactly what it is for — before you touch any platform. This spec sheet walks you through the decisions every builder will ask you to make anyway, then generates a portable plan you can paste into a Custom GPT, a Gem, a Claude Project, or a Copilot agent without re-thinking a thing.
