Agentic Browsing Brief

An agentic browser is only as safe as the instructions you hand it. A vague request — "find me some readings" — invites the agent to wander, click the wrong things, and act on whatever a page tells it. A brief does the opposite: it states the goal, draws the boundaries, and names the steps you must approve before they happen. This tool walks you through scoping one real task, then generates a portable brief you can paste into Comet, Atlas, or Auto-Browse.

Saved automatically on this device.

Why the brief comes first: The difference between a useful agent and a dangerous one is rarely the model — it's the instructions. A one-sentence goal, a named playing field, and an explicit list of "ask me first" steps convert delegation from a leap of faith into a supervised task. If a task can't be bounded this cleanly, that's a signal to do it yourself.
