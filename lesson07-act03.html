Part two is the pre-flight checklist every delegation should clear before the browser is allowed to act. None of this replaces your judgment — it structures it.

The honest default: If a task can't clear the checklist — especially the sensitive-data and supervision boxes — the right move isn't a different engine, it's to do it yourself.
