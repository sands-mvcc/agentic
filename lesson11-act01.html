FERPA Risk Audit: Map an AI Workflow

An AI workflow is only as compliant as its weakest link. In this lab you'll take a real-looking agent workflow, decide whether each tool may even hold student data, then map its FERPA exposure and attach a control to every step — a risk matrix you could hand your department.

The scenario

Maple Valley College pilots an "Advising Assistant" built from an agentic browser:

  • Node A — logs into the SIS and reads each advisee's transcript and contact info.
  • Node B — combines GPA, major, and directory info (name, email) into one profile.
  • Node C — drafts a "stay or switch majors" recommendation for each student.
  • Node D — emails the recommendation directly to the student.

Important: it runs on a faculty member's personal AI account — no institutional agreement.

Takeaway: Before you tune the details, ask the disqualifying question — is this tool even allowed to receive student data? Everything else is downstream of that.
